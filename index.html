What each map does

  • Geocodes the address (or takes coordinates) and pulls nearby records from EPA (ECHO, RCRA, SEMS/Superfund, TRI, air and water permits) and TCEQ (leaking and registered petroleum storage tanks, tanks in use from delivery certificates, state Superfund, Voluntary Cleanup, Innocent Owner, Brownfields, dry cleaners, solid waste and landfills, industrial and hazardous waste, Edwards Aquifer zones).
  • Measures distance, compass direction and relative elevation for every record, and flags what falls inside ASTM E1527-21 minimum search distances.
  • Gives you filters, a sortable table, key findings, a list of unmapped (orphan) records, and CSV / PDF export.

This is a screening aid built on public data. It doesn't replace a standard environmental database report or a professional's judgment under ASTM E1527-21.
